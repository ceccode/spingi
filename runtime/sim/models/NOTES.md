# Simulation models

## unitree_g1

Copied from https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie (folder `unitree_g1`, BSD-3 license by Unitree Robotics, see `LICENSE`). Only `g1.xml`, `scene.xml` and `assets/` are kept. Our changes with respect to the original:

- `g1.xml`: added the `head` camera on the `torso_link` body (position 0.06, 0, 0.40 in the torso frame, looking forward), used by `SimAdapter.get_camera`.

The `_gen_*.xml` files in this folder are scenes generated by `spingi.adapters.sim_mujoco.scene` and are ignored by git.
